Privacy

Last updated: October 21, 2026.

This website is operated by Travis Sweeter for commercial office-cleaning inquiries in the greater St. Cloud, Minnesota area.

Information collected

If you request a quote, you may provide your name, business name, phone number, approximate office size, preferred cleaning frequency, and details you choose to include.

How information is used

I use submitted information only to respond to your inquiry, evaluate whether the job is a good fit, arrange a walkthrough, prepare a quote, and communicate about requested cleaning services.

Form processing

The quote form is processed through FormSubmit so the inquiry can be delivered to my email. Do not submit confidential financial, medical, account, access-code, alarm-code, or other sensitive information through the form.

Tracking

The site does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. A simple source label may identify whether a quote request came from a printed business-card QR code or the website directly.
